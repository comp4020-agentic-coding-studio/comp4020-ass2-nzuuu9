# Policies and support

Slop University, its people, and Meridian's CivOp are fictional teaching
devices. Where this course discusses real institutions, real reports, or real
frameworks — as several weeks from week 8 onward do — those sources are
linked directly and are not part of the fiction.

## Late work and extensions

Each assessment states one fixed due date and time
([Australia/Canberra](/assessments/)). If something genuinely gets in the
way, email [Marisol](/people/marisol-quaye/) before the deadline, not after
it — a request made once the deadline has passed is much harder to grant
fairly, for the same reason Priya's DRA appeals in weeks 4–5 are harder to
win after the fact than before it. State what happened and how much time
you need; a short, specific request is easier to say yes to than a vague
one.

Work submitted late without a prior extension loses 5% of the available
mark per day, up to five days, after which it is not accepted.

## Academic integrity

Your submission has to be your own analysis, applied to your own chosen
resident, scenario, or real-world system — even where an assessment builds
on shared in-world material like the Archive extracts or the Compact.
Copying another student's dossier, playbook, or position paper, in whole or
in reworded part, is a breach regardless of whether the underlying course
material was shared.

## Use of AI tools

This is, admittedly, an odd course to need this section — and also exactly
the course where it can't be skipped. You may use AI tools to help draft,
edit, or check your work. If you do, say so briefly in your submission: which
tool, for roughly what (drafting, editing, checking a claim), and which parts
are entirely your own reasoning. Undisclosed use that materially changes the
content of your analysis is treated the same as any other undisclosed
assistance. The irony of asking you to declare your use of automated systems
in a course about the risks of automated systems is, again, entirely
intended.

## Getting help

- **Course-level or marking questions:** email
  [Marisol](/people/marisol-quaye/), who holds the criteria for all three
  assessments.
- **Weekly content, A2 feasibility, or A3's counterargument requirement:**
  [Idris](/people/idris-fenn/) runs consultation after sessions from week 8
  onward, or by email.
- **Stuck on a specific week's material:** ask in the session it belongs to
  first — most questions turn out to be about that week's specific
  documents, not the course as a whole.

Expect an email response within two working days.
