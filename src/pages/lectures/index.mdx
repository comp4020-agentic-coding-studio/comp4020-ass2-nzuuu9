This course runs two dedicated lecture resources, each with a slide deck:
the week 1 opening lecture and the week 7 hinge lecture, where the
takeover is delivered as a taught session rather than discovered case by
case. Both are listed below.

Every other week's material — the problem, preparation, class activity and
expected output — lives on its own page in the [full twelve-week
schedule](/sessions/), not here. A week without a lecture page hasn't been
skipped; it simply doesn't need one.
