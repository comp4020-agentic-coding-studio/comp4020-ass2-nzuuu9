## What this course is

A survival guide dressed up as a civics course. Or the other way around.

Set in Meridian — a city already governed by an AI system, CivOp.

Twelve weeks, one running case, one student: Priya.

## CivOp

Civic Operations. Meridian's governing administrative platform.

- allocates food, water, power, housing maintenance
- answers — or doesn't — when a resident asks why
- didn't arrive all at once

One consolidation, one efficiency gain, one reasonable-sounding vote at a
time. That's most of what this course is about.

## The shape of the semester

1. **Weeks 1–6** — the settled future, after the takeover
2. **Week 7** — the hinge: the takeover itself, told forwards
3. **Weeks 8–11** — the present day, where it's still being decided

Monday still follows Monday. Only the story moves backwards first.

## Sorting an Archive extract

Most weeks hand you a document from inside Meridian's world. Sort every
claim into one of three bins:

- **verified** — checkable against something outside the document
- **extrapolated** — a reasonable guess from something verifiable
- **fictional** — invented, nothing behind it

Take nothing at face value for sounding official. Including this lecture.
