**Monday 8 March 2027 — story time: far future, Meridian.**

## The resident's problem

Weeks 1 and 2 gave you an annotated source table and a dependency diagram.
Neither, on its own, tells a stranger what it's actually like to be Priya
this week. That's what a dossier is for: one coherent, sourced account of
a resident's ordinary day under CivOp, built from exactly the material
you've already produced, not from scratch.

## Learning goals

By the end of this session you should be able to:

- Combine separately-produced evidence — a source table, a dependency
  map — into one document that reads as a single argument rather than
  three attachments.
- Apply the verified / extrapolated / fictional tagging discipline
  consistently across a longer piece of writing, not just isolated claims.

## Preparation and materials

Bring your week 1 source table and week 2 dependency diagram, and read the
[A1 — City Dossier brief](/assessments/assignment-1/) in full before the
session — specifically the marking criteria, which is what this session's
workshop checks your draft against.

## Class activity

Swap drafts with a partner. For each other's dossier, check three things
in order: first, that every claim about Meridian carries a tag; second,
that every claim about access or exclusion names a specific CivOp or DRA
mechanism rather than gesturing at "the system"; third, that a reader with
no prior knowledge of Meridian could follow the account unaided. Note
anything that fails any of the three directly on the draft, then swap
back and discuss.

## Expected output

A revised dossier draft, incorporating your partner's notes, ready for
Thursday's submission. Nothing new is invented this week — the work is
turning weeks 1–2 into one document that would survive a stranger's
scrutiny.
