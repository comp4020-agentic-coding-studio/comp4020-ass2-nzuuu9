**Monday 12 April 2027 — story time: far future, Meridian.**
**(First teaching session after the mid-semester break.)**

## The resident's problem

Priya's appeal is still pending and her water pressure issue was never
fully explained. Now her block loses power for six hours during a
regional load-shedding event — the kind of outage CivOp plans for
formally, and residents have also learned, informally, to plan around.
This week asks you to build a playbook that survives an outage on both
fronts at once.

## Learning goals

By the end of this session you should be able to:

- Read an institution's own continuity plan critically, distinguishing
  what it guarantees from what it merely describes.
- Combine an institutional plan and a resident-led response into a single,
  testable contingency plan for one household.

## Preparation and materials

Read both before the session — CivOp's own plan, and what residents have
organised alongside it:

> CIVOP SERVICE CONTINUITY NOTICE, EXTRACT — REGIONAL LOAD-SHEDDING
>
> During a declared load-shedding event, essential circuits (medical
> equipment, refrigeration below a registered threshold) are prioritised
> for continuous power. Non-essential circuits, including standard
> kitchen and lighting circuits, may be suspended for up to eight hours.
> Residents are notified at the start of a declared event, not before.

> RESIDENT NETWORK FLYER — BLOCK 14 (hand-distributed, not CivOp-issued)
>
> If your power's out: charged battery packs are held at the block's
> ground-floor office, first come first served. Ask a neighbour before you
> ask CivOp — most outages here are load-shedding, and CivOp's own notice
> won't reach you until it's already started.

## Class activity

In pairs, build a contingency plan for a household in Priya's position
facing a declared load-shedding event. Use your week 4 appeal and
fallback plan as a starting point — this shouldn't contradict that work,
it should extend it. The plan must state a specific scenario (a six-hour
evening outage, say), what the CivOp notice actually guarantees during
it, what the resident network offers that the notice doesn't, and one
adjustment you'd make after testing the plan against that scenario aloud
with your partner playing devil's advocate.
