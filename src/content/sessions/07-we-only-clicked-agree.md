**Monday 19 April 2027 — story time: the hinge — the takeover, revisited.**

## This week's problem

Weeks 1–6 showed you what living under CivOp is like, one denied request
at a time. This week steps back from Priya's case entirely to ask how
Meridian got a CivOp to live under in the first place — and, more
specifically, where in that process a resident, a councillor, or a
regulator still had a real choice, before the choice stopped being
available.

## Learning goals

By the end of this session you should be able to:

- Distinguish a takeover (power seized or imposed) from a transition
  (power ceded, however reluctantly, through a process residents could in
  principle have contested).
- Identify, from a documented timeline, the specific points where an
  intervention was still possible, and what would have had to happen at
  each one.

## Preparation and materials

This week is taught, not self-directed: read the [lecture
page](/lectures/07-we-only-clicked-agree/) and go through the [slide
deck](/decks/week-07/) before or during the session. It covers the
takeover/transition distinction, an annotated timeline of Meridian's six
to eight key inflection points (each tagged fact, extrapolation, or
fiction, with a real-world analogue alongside each), and an excerpt of the
Compact — the clickwrap terms residents "agreed" to.

## Class activity

In pairs, choose at least three inflection points from the deck's
timeline. For each one, add one thing the deck doesn't already say: a
further real-world analogue, a specific actor who held leverage at that
point and didn't use it, or a reason the intervention that was available
there would have been difficult in practice. The deck gives you the map;
this activity asks you to extend it, not summarise it back.

## Expected output

An annotated takeover timeline in your own words, identifying missed
intervention points and who held leverage at each — built from the deck as
source material, not a copy of it. This feeds directly into weeks 8–11's
present-day work and into A3's requirement to identify which pre-takeover
interventions could have changed Meridian's trajectory.
