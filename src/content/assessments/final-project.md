## The brief

> Identify which pre-takeover interventions could have changed Meridian's
> trajectory, and defend one concrete direction for the present day.

This is the capstone the whole course has been building towards. Weeks
1–6 gave you a resident's case in detail; week 7 gave you a timeline of
where Meridian's takeover could have gone differently; weeks 8–11 grounded
all of it in present-day governance levers, oversight rules, resource
costs, and a workshopped intervention. A3 asks you to bring that material
together into a position: given everything you now know about how a
system like CivOp comes to exist and what it costs to run, what should
happen — to Meridian, and by extension to real present-day AI
governance?

There is no single correct position. Continued AI governance, reform,
ending it outright, or another direction entirely all score identically
well, provided the direction is argued through its practical consequences
and provided you state the strongest case against your own position
fairly — in terms its proponents would recognise, not a version easy to
knock down — before answering it. A position paper that never engages a
real counterargument cannot score well on this brief no matter which
position it takes.

## What you submit

A position paper (the format is yours to choose — a structured essay, an
annotated brief, a memo — provided it reads as a complete, stand-alone
argument). It must name specific evidence from your own coursework: at
least one intervention point from your week 7 timeline, material from
your A1 and A2 submissions, and at least one real-world data point from
weeks 8–10 (a governance lever, an oversight rule, a resource-cost
figure). Tag claims about Meridian the same way A1 required: verified,
extrapolated, or fictional.

## How it's marked

See the table above. Agreement with any particular political conclusion
is not assessed — a well-argued case for continuity scores the same as a
well-argued case for dismantling CivOp entirely. Comedic or entertainment
value is not assessed either way.
