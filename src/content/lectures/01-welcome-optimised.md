  already governed by an AI system, told in reverse.

  timeline explained, with the four-stage map (far future → hinge →
  present day → open) repeated from the homepage.

  why none of it should be taken at face value, including this lecture.

  page](/sessions/01-welcome-optimised/) for the extract and the task).
